Diesel Mechanic FAQs

What diesel vehicles do you service? +
We service all makes and models of diesel vehicles, including utes, 4WDs, SUVs, passenger cars, and light commercial vehicles. Popular brands include Toyota, Ford Ranger, Mitsubishi Triton, Isuzu DMAX, Nissan Navara, Mazda BT50, Volkswagen, and MercedesBenz.
My DPF warning light is on what does that mean? +
A DPF (Diesel Particulate Filter) warning light means the filter is becoming blocked with soot. In some cases a forced regeneration or professional clean can clear it. Ignoring it can lead to expensive damage. Bring it in and we'll diagnose the issue properly.
How often should a diesel engine be serviced? +
Most diesel vehicles are serviced every 10,00015,000 km or 12 months, but this varies by make and model. Check your logbook for the manufacturer's recommended interval. Diesel engines in particular benefit from regular oil and filter changes to protect injectors and turbos.
Can you service my diesel under its new car warranty? +
Yes. Under Australian Consumer Law, you can have your vehicle serviced by any qualified mechanic without voiding the manufacturer's warranty, provided the service meets manufacturer specifications which ours does.
My diesel is rough to start or has lost power what could it be? +
Common causes include worn glow plugs, dirty or failing injectors, a blocked fuel filter, or turbo issues. We'll run a full diagnostic to identify the root cause before recommending any repairs.
