FAQs

Why does my car keep needing repairs? +
Frequent repairs often mean deeper issues like worn components, skipped services or poor past workmanship. We'll diagnose the root cause – not just treat the symptoms.
How long does a standard service take? +
Most logbook services are completed same day. We'll let you know upfront if extra work is needed.
Do you service fleet or company vehicles? +
Absolutely. We're authorised to service vehicles managed by Fleet Card, Maxxia, AVC, Smartfleet and others.
Can I keep my warranty with a local mechanic? +
Yes. Our logbook services follow all manufacturer specifications, so your warranty stays intact.
Do you work on older cars with carburettors? +
We do! In fact, it's one of our specialities. We're one of the few workshops still experienced in carburettor tuning and repairs.
