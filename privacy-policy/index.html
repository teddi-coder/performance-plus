Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. About This Policy

Performance Plus ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and store your personal information when you use our website or engage our automotive workshop services at E/20 Endurance Ave, Queanbeyan NSW 2620.

By using our website or providing us with your personal information, you consent to its collection and use as described in this policy.

2. What Personal Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of personal information:

  • Your name and contact details (phone number, email address, postal address)
  • Vehicle information (make, model, year, registration)
  • Service history and repair records
  • Payment and billing information
  • Communications you send us (including enquiry form submissions)
  • Website usage data collected via cookies and analytics tools (see Section 7)

We only collect personal information that is reasonably necessary to provide our services and respond to your enquiries.

3. How We Collect Personal Information

We collect personal information in the following ways:

  • Directly from you when you contact us by phone, email, or through our website contact/booking forms
  • When you bring your vehicle in for servicing or repairs
  • From fleet management providers (e.g. Maxxia, FleetPlus, Smartfleet, AVC, FleetCard) in relation to fleet servicing arrangements
  • Automatically through website analytics tools (see Section 7)

4. How We Use Your Personal Information

We use your personal information to:

  • Provide automotive servicing, repairs, and inspections
  • Respond to your enquiries and booking requests
  • Send service reminders and follow-up communications (where you have consented)
  • Process payments and maintain service records
  • Comply with our legal and regulatory obligations (e.g. NSW eSafety check requirements)
  • Improve our services and website

We will not use your personal information for any purpose that you would not reasonably expect, or in a manner inconsistent with this policy.

5. Disclosure of Personal Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may disclose your information to:

  • Transport for NSW, when submitting eSafety inspection (pink slip) results as required by law
  • Fleet management providers, in connection with authorised fleet servicing arrangements
  • Our service providers who assist us in operating our business (e.g. booking software, payment processors), under confidentiality obligations
  • Law enforcement or government agencies where required or authorised by law

6. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from misuse, interference, loss, unauthorised access, modification, or disclosure. Service records are stored securely at our workshop premises. Electronic information is protected by appropriate technical measures.

While we take reasonable precautions, no data transmission over the internet can be guaranteed as completely secure. You provide information online at your own risk.

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law.

7. Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website uses Google Tag Manager and associated tools (including Google Analytics) to collect anonymous data about how visitors use our site. This may include your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on the site. This data is used in aggregate form to improve our website and marketing.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Most browsers also allow you to control cookies through their settings.

8. Access and Correction

Under the Privacy Act, you have the right to access the personal information we hold about you and to request corrections if it is inaccurate, incomplete, or out of date. To make a request, please contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable time and at no charge in most circumstances.

9. Complaints

If you believe we have breached your privacy rights, please contact us first so we can try to resolve the issue. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at www.oaic.gov.au.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will always be available on this page with the date last updated noted at the top. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
