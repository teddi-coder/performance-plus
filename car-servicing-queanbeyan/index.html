What Is a Car Service?

Regular car servicing keeps your vehicle safe, reliable, and running efficiently. A service checks and replaces key components before they become problems — saving you money on bigger repairs down the track.

At Performance Plus, our experienced technicians carry out every service thoroughly and explain exactly what was done and why. No surprises, no upselling.

  • Engine diagnostics and fault checks
  • Clear explanation of any findings

Car Servicing FAQs

How often should I service my car? +
Most vehicles need a service every 10,000–15,000 km or every 12 months — whichever comes first. Check your owner's manual for your manufacturer's recommended interval. If you're unsure, give us a call and we can advise based on your make and model.
What's the difference between a minor and major service? +
A minor service typically covers an oil and filter change, fluid top-ups, and a basic safety check. A major service is more comprehensive — it includes spark plugs, air filters, brake fluid, coolant, and a full mechanical inspection. We'll recommend the right service for your vehicle's age and mileage.
Do you service all makes and models? +
Yes. We service all passenger vehicles — Japanese, European, American, and Australian makes, petrol and diesel. If you're not sure whether we can help with your specific vehicle, just give us a call on 02 6324 1091.
Will you tell me if you find other issues? +
Always. If we find anything that needs attention during the service, we'll let you know what it is and what it'll cost before we do any extra work. No surprises on your invoice.
How long does a car service take? +
A minor service typically takes 1–2 hours. A major service can take half a day or more depending on the vehicle and what's involved. We'll give you an estimated time when you book so you can plan accordingly.
